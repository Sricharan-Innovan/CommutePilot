    Questions.

    Which cities does it cover?

    Hyderabad Metro is mapped station by station today — all 59 stations across the Red, Blue and Green lines, with the interchanges. Bus stops, offices and landmarks work anywhere, because those legs use a distance geofence rather than a station sequence. More metro networks are being added line by line.

    Is there an app to install?

    No. CommutePilot is a Telegram bot, so it uses an app you already have, with notifications you already trust. Open the chat, run the setup once, and it works on any phone that runs Telegram.

    What about my battery?

    There is no extra app running in the background all day. During a trip you share live location from Telegram for the hour, which the operating system already optimises, and it ends by itself when the trip does.

    Does it work underground?

    Hyderabad Metro runs elevated, so GPS holds for the whole ride. On underground sections elsewhere, position goes stale in the tunnel and picks up again at the next elevated stretch or station — which is why the alert is tied to the preceding station rather than a precise metre count.

    Do I have to keep the chat open?

    No. Lock the phone and put it away. The alerts arrive as notifications, with sound and vibration, like any other message.

    What happens on a day I do not travel?

    Tap WFH or On leave on the morning prompt and the day goes quiet, evening included. Snooze 30 pushes the prompt back half an hour if you are simply running late.

    What do you keep about me?

    Your stops, your times and the days you travel — the things the alerts need. Live location is used while a trip is running and is not kept as a history. No ads, and nothing sold on.
